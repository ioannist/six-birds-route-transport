# Paper evidence pack

## Purpose

This memo defines the stable paper-facing evidence bundle used for drafting.
The bundle is grounded only in committed theorem-side materials, committed axis
assets, committed witness assets, and committed paper-facing summary assets
under `experiments/instances/paper/`.

The pack does not depend on missing transient runtime outputs from:

- TH6 hierarchy synthesis under `results/results/...three_axis_hierarchy...`
- T50 flagship control runs under `results/results/...flagship_control_bundle...`

Instead, those gaps are handled by committed paper-facing summary substitutions.

## Primary theorem-side evidence

Primary theorem-side evidence is the material that directly fixes the paper's
theorem object, comparison base, and claim interpretation:

- `vision.md`
- `docs/runbooks/three-axis-context-memo.md`
- `docs/runbooks/frozen-slice-shared-support-layer.md`
- `docs/runbooks/hierarchy-proposition-layer.md`
- `docs/runbooks/package-conflict-sharpening.md`

These are the drafting anchors for:

- the event-package theorem object
- admissible global packaging
- frozen-slice/shared-support comparison
- hierarchy non-implication and adjudicated comparison rules
- sharpened package-conflict interpretation

## Flagship numerical evidence

The stable flagship list in `experiments/instances/paper/flagship-witnesses.json`
collects the paper-facing numerical anchors:

- explicit parity/context benchmark witness
- committed mechanism-axis quotient-backed witness
- committed lens-axis cross-resolution accepted obstruction
- committed packaging-axis accepted flagship with package-conflict adjudication

This list preserves claim ceilings and does not collapse axis-wide outcomes into
single witness labels.

## Supporting controls

The stable control-facing artifact is
`experiments/instances/paper/flagship-control-summary.json`.
This is a committed summary substitution for the missing T50 runtime folder. It
records:

- per-case hidden-record verdict
- per-case flattening verdict
- per-case robustness verdict
- per-case final verdict
- bundle-level verdict
- quotient-backed pre/post summaries

The current control story is therefore stable and drafting-ready without
pointing at missing runtime files.

## Key caveats

The stable caveat registry is
`experiments/instances/paper/caveat-registry.json`.
It preserves at minimum:

- mechanism campaign inadequacy versus committed witness
- lens same-step bounded-negative subregime versus accepted cross-resolution flagship
- packaging selector-branch / fixed-operator-family-source caveat
- flagship control applicability caveat
- bounded finite-family scope caveat
