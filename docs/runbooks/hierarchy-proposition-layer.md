# Hierarchy proposition layer

## Preface

The theorem object of the paper remains the event package
\[
\mathfrak E = (\mathcal C,\{B_c\},\sim,\mathcal A),
\]
with admissible global packaging as the central realization problem. A short
proposition layer is useful because the current hierarchy evidence is not a
flat set of interchangeable contextuality claims. The propositions below state
what follows formally, what does not follow, and what comparison rule is
adjudicated by the present theory plus committed evidence.

## Proposition 1

**Statement.** Mechanism change does not by itself imply lens incompatibility.

**Kind.** `non_implication`

**Argument / evidence sketch.** The hierarchy memo and frozen-slice layer place
mechanism variation outside the default strongest same-system comparison object:
mechanism usually changes the outer theory-point or support regime, while
lens-side incompatibility is a claim about record-algebra relations on one fixed
support. The committed mechanism campaign summary supports mechanism dependence
and package-surface regime selection, but its own claim ceiling remains below
the stronger same-system lens claim. The committed mechanism witness therefore
does not collapse the hierarchy by itself.

## Proposition 2

**Statement.** Lens incompatibility does not by itself imply packaging obstruction.

**Kind.** `non_implication`

**Argument / evidence sketch.** The same-step lens subregime establishes that
fixed-support record-algebra variation can generate nontrivial structure,
candidate-subset quotient witnesses, and weak frustration without producing an
accepted obstruction. Packaging obstruction is therefore a stronger statement
than mere lens-side non-nestedness or incompatibility pressure.

## Proposition 3

**Statement.** Packaging obstruction implies no admissible global package.

**Kind.** `formal_consequence`

**Argument / evidence sketch.** Under the current theorem object and quotient
backend semantics, an accepted packaging obstruction is precisely a failure of
admissible global realization for the event package on the relevant comparison
base. So this implication is not merely empirical. It is part of what
packaging-level obstruction means in the paper.

## Proposition 4

**Statement.** Same-support cross-resolution strict extension can still count as
a valid lens/package comparison under a fixed evaluation regime.

**Kind.** `adjudicated_rule`

**Argument / evidence sketch.** The package-change paper states that comparison
must be made on a frozen slice and against a fixed evaluation regime rather than
a moving ledger. The frozen-slice layer then makes the support object explicit.
Within that regime, cross-resolution comparison is legitimate when it changes
the record algebra or package map while preserving the fixed support object and
evaluation regime. The committed lens cross-resolution closure is the working
repo instance of that rule.

## Synthesis

Together these four propositions clarify the ordering of the hierarchy.
Mechanism is outermost and does not automatically entail the stronger
same-support record-algebra claims. Lens variation can create genuine strict
extension and incompatibility pressure, but that still does not automatically
upgrade to packaging obstruction. Packaging obstruction is strongest because it
is the theorem-facing failure of admissible global packaging itself. The
frozen-slice layer explains why strongest same-system claims require a fixed
comparison base, and why the current evidence must not be collapsed into one
undifferentiated contextuality label.
